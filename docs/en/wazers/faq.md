# Driver FAQ

## Application

**Where can I get help with the application?**

Waze uses Google's support pages. Check their [official help page](https://support.google.com/waze), or ask your questions on the [Community Forum](https://www.waze.com/discuss/c/editors/switzerland/4790).

## Swiss motorways

**When searching for a route through Switzerland, the application avoids motorways, why?**

Swiss motorways require a pass called the "vignette". It costs CHF 40.- and must be stuck on the windshield. Without a vignette, the fine is CHF 200.-. Waze needs a confirmation that you own the vignette in order to suggest motorways in Switzerland. See [Motorway vignette](/en/wazers/vignette).

## Road closures

**A major event or roadworks will affect traffic in my area, how can I help?**

Inform the local community. Post on the [Waze Switzerland Forum](https://www.waze.com/discuss/c/editors/switzerland/4790), or send an e-mail to fermetures@waze-switzerland.ch (fr/it/en) or sperrungen@waze-switzerland.ch (de/en).

## Speed cameras

**Why are no fixed speed cameras reported on Waze in Switzerland?**

Speed cameras have been disabled due to Swiss law. The feature will not be re-enabled unless the law changes.

## Fuel prices

**The fuel prices are wrong, what should I do?**

The only way to update prices is to do it from the application, within a 500 m radius around the station: orange report button > fuel prices.

**I reported wrong prices but nothing was corrected, why?**

Map editors cannot update prices remotely: only people located less than 500 m from the station can do it via the application. Editors therefore close these reports without any action possible on their side.
