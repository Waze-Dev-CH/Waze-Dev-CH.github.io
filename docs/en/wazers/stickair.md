# Stick'AIR: Geneva

<img class="macaron" src="/img/wazers/stickair-1.png" alt="Stick'AIR sticker (categories 0 to 5)">

::: note Specific to the canton of Geneva
This feature only applies to the Geneva region.
:::

The canton of Geneva applies a **differentiated traffic** system based on the **Stick'AIR** environmental sticker. When the authorities activate traffic restrictions (during a pollution peak), Waze automatically calculates alternative routes to avoid the zones prohibited for your vehicle category.

## Configuring your Stick'AIR category

1. Open **Settings** in the Waze app
2. Go to **Add a toll/carpool pass**
3. Select your **Stick'AIR** category (0, 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5) according to your vehicle

<div class="media-row">
<img class="app-shot" src="/img/wazers/stickair-4.png" alt="'Toll and carpool stickers' list with Stick'AIR categories 0 to 5">
</div>

Once configured, Waze adapts the routes and ETAs based on the restrictions in force.

## Frequently asked questions

**What happens if I don't add a sticker?**

Waze guides you around the restricted zone, or warns you of a risk of a fine if your destination is located inside it.

**I already have the French Crit'Air, do I need to configure Stick'AIR separately?**

No. The Geneva system recognizes French Crit'Air stickers, which are automatically valid for route calculation in Geneva.
