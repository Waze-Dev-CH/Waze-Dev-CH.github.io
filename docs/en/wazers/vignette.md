# Swiss motorway vignette

<img class="banner-img is-narrow" src="/img/wazers/vignette-1.png" alt="Waze and the Swiss motorway vignette">

::: important Important
The vignette is **mandatory** on all Swiss motorways. The fine for not having one is **CHF 200**.
:::

Waze now includes the Swiss motorway vignette in its route calculations. Previously, the app could only allow or block toll roads globally, without distinguishing between the toll systems of different countries. You can therefore tell the app that you have a Swiss vignette while still avoiding tolls elsewhere, which is useful for cross-border trips in Europe.

<img class="banner-img" src="/img/wazers/vignette-3.jpg" alt="Map of the European motorway network in Waze: vignette countries and toll countries">


The Swiss motorway vignette costs **CHF 40 per year** and must be affixed to the vehicle's windscreen. Without confirmation in Waze, the app automatically avoids motorways when calculating routes.

## Enabling motorway routing in Waze

1. Open **Settings** in the Waze app
2. Go to **Navigation**
3. Select **Add toll/carpool pass**
4. Choose **Vignette CH** and confirm

<div class="media-row">
<img class="app-shot" src="/img/wazers/vignette-2.png" alt="Waze 'Passes' screen with the Vignette CH option to add">
</div>

Once enabled, Waze will use Swiss motorways in its route calculations.

::: warning
Changing this setting in Waze **does not replace the obligation to buy the vignette**. The purchase must be made separately through official channels.
:::

## Buying the vignette

- [e-vignette.ch](https://e-vignette.ch/): official digital vignette
- [via.admin.ch/shop](https://via.admin.ch/shop): official shop of the Swiss Confederation

## Testing routing with the vignette

You can test a route with the vignette enabled directly on the [Waze Live Map](https://www.waze.com/live-map/directions?rp_subscription=vignette-switzerland).
