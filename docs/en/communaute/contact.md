# Contact

At Waze, Switzerland is part of the **DACH** group (Germany, Austria, Switzerland). Communication between users and the community happens mainly on the **Waze forums** and on **Discord**. To report a map error to the editors, write to **report@waze-switzerland.ch**. For a planned **road closure** (event, roadworks), see the [Report a road closure](#report-a-road-closure) section below.

## The Champs

The Swiss community is led by the **Champs** (coordinators). Click on an address to write to them; they are assembled by the browser to limit automated harvesting.

<ChampsGrid />

## Report a road closure

To report a road closure to the editing team:

- **fermetures@waze-switzerland.ch**: messages in French, Italian or English
- **sperrungen@waze-switzerland.ch**: messages in German or English

::: reminder Reminder
These addresses are meant for planned closures and roadworks. For one-off incidents, use the in-app reporting directly in Waze.
:::
