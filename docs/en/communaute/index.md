# Swiss Waze Community

The Swiss Waze community is part of the **DACH** area (Germany, Austria, Switzerland). It brings together drivers and volunteer editors around a forum, a Discord and mentoring roles. This site is community-managed: it is not affiliated with Waze/Google.

## In this section

- [Cafés & Meetups](/en/communaute/cafes-meetups): community gatherings (Waze Cafés, DACH meetups)
- [Contact](/en/communaute/contact): Swiss coordinators (Champs), e-mails and channels
- [Partnerships](/en/communaute/partenariats): official Waze programs (cities, events, tunnels)

## Editing the map

For everything related to editing (rules, editing forum, resources, WME roles), see the [Editors](/en/editors/) section and in particular [Editors community](/en/editors/communaute/).
