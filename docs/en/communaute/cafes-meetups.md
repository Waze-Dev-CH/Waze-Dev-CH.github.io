# Cafés & Meetups

<img class="banner-img" src="/img/communaute/cafe-1.png" alt="Waze Café: map of the cantons of French-speaking Switzerland">

The Swiss Waze community meets regularly at local and regional events, in two main formats: Waze Cafés and Local Meetups.

## Waze Cafés

Waze Cafés are small, informal events (10-15 participants) organized by the local community. Editors from the region gather to collaborate on editing or community-life topics, share best practices, and meet outside the online forums.

**Waze Café Romandie**: organized by the French-speaking community. The last gathering took place in October 2025 in Geneva. The next 2026 edition is being planned; the date and location are still to be determined.

::: note Note
There is not yet an equivalent initiative on the German-speaking or Ticino side. Feel free to propose one via Discord or the forum.
:::

## Waze Local Meetups

<img class="banner-img" src="/img/communaute/cafe-2.png" alt="Waze Local Meetups banner">

Waze Local Meetups are annual two-day events (about 8 hours of program) that bring together new and experienced members around the major community topics: editing, tools, governance, and exchanges between regions.

<img class="photo" src="/img/communaute/cafe-3.png" alt="Group photo from a DACH Local Meetup">

## D-A-CH Local Meetups

D-A-CH Local Meetups are joint regional gatherings bringing together the communities of Switzerland, Germany, and Austria.

Recent editions:

| Edition | Location | Date |
|---------|------|------|
| 2025 | Basel (CH) | June 2025 |
| 2024 | Vienna (AT) | September 2024 |
| 2023 | Cologne (DE) | June 2023 |
| 2022 | Bern (CH) | May 2022 |

The next 2026 edition is still to be determined. Follow the announcements on the [community Discord](https://discord.gg/dmxUwvTkk5) and the Waze Switzerland forum.
