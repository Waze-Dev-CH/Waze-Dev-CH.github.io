# Mapping

This section brings together the editing rules specific to Switzerland. They complement the general Waze rules and take precedence over the guides of neighbouring countries.

::: important Important
Mapping rules vary by country. In Switzerland, local rules always apply: never transpose a French, German or Italian rule without verification.
:::

## Why Swiss rules?

Switzerland is multilingual, uses its own road classification (A, N1–N30, H1–…) and has unique legal specificities (motorway vignette, ban on speed camera alerts).
