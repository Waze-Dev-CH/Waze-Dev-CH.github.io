# Useful resources

This page gathers the external links and useful tools for editing the Waze map in Switzerland. When in doubt, local resources (Swiss Wiki, forum, Discord) always take precedence over improvisation.

## Wikis and Wazeopedia

The German-language Wiki is the reference for editing in Switzerland, since the French translations may lag behind.

- Wazeopedia Switzerland: https://www.waze.com/discuss/c/wazeopedia/switzerland-wazeopedia/5228
- French-language hub (Informations en français): https://www.waze.com/discuss/t/informations-en-francais/377254
- German-language hub (Hauptseite): https://www.waze.com/discuss/t/hauptseite/377253

::: note Languages
The French-language hub gathers the pages translated for French-speaking Switzerland. The Hauptseite remains the main page on the DACH side.
:::

## Forum and community

- Switzerland category of the Waze forum: https://www.waze.com/discuss/c/editors/switzerland/4790
- Romandie forum: https://www.waze.com/forum/viewforum.php?f=836
- Community Discord: https://discord.gg/dmxUwvTkk5

::: help Where to ask a question
The Romandie forum (pinned topics) and the community Discord are the right places to ask before editing when you are unsure.
:::
