# Community

The Swiss Waze community is part of the **DACH** zone (Germany, Austria, Switzerland). It is organized around a forum, a Discord and mentoring roles.

## Where to ask a question

Before asking a question, check the Wiki and the forum: the answer is often already there.

- **Forum Romandie**, editing questions for French-speaking Switzerland (pinned topics): https://www.waze.com/discuss/c/editors/switzerland/romandie/4797
- **Community Discord**, direct exchanges: https://discord.gg/dmxUwvTkk5
- **CH community site**: [waze-switzerland.ch](https://www.waze-switzerland.ch/)

::: note Note
Each linguistic region has its own rules. For French-speaking Switzerland, follow the local rules, not those of France. For German-speaking Switzerland, the German-language Wiki is the reference.
:::
