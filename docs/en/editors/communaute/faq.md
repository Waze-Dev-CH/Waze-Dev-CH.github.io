# FAQ: Frequently asked questions

## Waze application

**On which platforms is Waze available?**
iOS and Android.

**Where can I get help with the application?**
Through the [official Google help pages](https://support.google.com/waze) (in English) or on the [Swiss community forum](https://www.waze.com/discuss/c/editors/switzerland/romandie/4797).


## Map editor (WME)

**Who can edit the map?**
Anyone with a Waze account and a valid email address can access the [Waze Map Editor](https://www.waze.com/editor).

**Which areas can I edit?**
The areas where you have driven with Waze over the **last 90 days**. Access rights expire if you do not drive on the road again within that period.

**How do I edit a segment locked at a higher level?**
You cannot edit it directly. Request the unlock through the community forum. Once approved, you can make the change.


## Swiss specifics (drivers)

Driver-side questions (**vignette and motorways**, **disabled speed cameras**, **fuel prices**, **reporting closures**) are grouped together in the [drivers' FAQ](/en/wazers/faq) to avoid duplication.

Useful reminder for editors: a UR reporting an incorrect **fuel price** must be closed as "technically impossible to fix by the editor": only drivers within 500 m of the station can update the price from the application.
