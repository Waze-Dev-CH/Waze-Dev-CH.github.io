# Editor roles

Beyond the editor level, the Swiss Waze community relies on specific roles that grant extended editing rights.

## Area Manager (AM)

An **Area Manager** manages a defined geographic area and can edit the map there without having physically driven on every road, within the limit of their lock level.

### Requirements to apply

- At least **500 significant edits**
- Good knowledge of Swiss editing rules
- Active participation in the forum (several contributions)
- Registered for **at least one month**

### Process

1. Open the WME and center the map on the desired area
2. Create one or more permalinks delimiting the requested area
3. Contact a Country Manager via the forum with these links
4. A Country Manager gets in touch within **24 hours**

::: important Important
Area Managers must edit the map **at least once every 90 days**. After this period, AM rights are automatically revoked.
:::

## State Manager (SM)

A **State Manager** has full editing rights in the assigned canton, subject to the same locking rules.

## RTC State Manager

A role specific to Switzerland (unique in this country): managing real-time road closures. See [Road closures](/en/editors/wme/fermetures) for details on the procedures.

## Country Manager (CM)

**Country Managers** provide support at the national level, enforce the rules and lead the community. They are the ones who grant AM and SM status.

::: note Up-to-date list
The list of CM, SM and AM names evolves. Refer to the dedicated section of the Swiss Wiki rather than relying on a frozen copy.
:::

For community questions, see [Community](/en/editors/communaute/).
