# Turn restrictions

Turn restrictions tell Waze which movements are allowed or prohibited at a junction. They have a direct impact on route calculation.

## Viewing restrictions

Select a segment to display the turn arrows at each end:

- **Green arrow** = turn allowed
- **Red arrow** = turn prohibited
- **No arrow** = no restriction recorded (allowed by default)

Click an arrow to toggle between allowed and prohibited.

## Basic rules

- Always check the actual signage (signs, road markings) before setting a restriction
- A legal but dangerous turn is not a sufficient reason to restrict it
- One-way streets imply automatic restrictions in the blocked direction

## Time-based restrictions

Some turns are prohibited only at certain times or on certain days (e.g.: no left turn during rush hour). To set them:

1. Select the relevant turn arrow
2. Click the clock icon that appears
3. Define the days and time ranges
4. Save with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd>

## Restrictions by vehicle type

You can restrict a turn only for certain vehicle types (trucks, motorcycles, etc.) by clicking "Edit types" in the restriction panel.

## U-turns

U-turns are handled as turn restrictions on the same segment. Only allow a U-turn when:
- The maneuver creates an actual dead end
- It is legal and signposted
