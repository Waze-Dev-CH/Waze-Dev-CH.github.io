# Map Editor (WME)

The **Waze Map Editor (WME)** is the online tool used to edit the Waze map. It is accessible to all users with a Waze account at [waze.com/editor](https://www.waze.com/editor).

## Access and editing rights

By driving with the Waze app, you accumulate **temporary editing rights** (valid for 90 days) over the areas you travel through. These rights determine where you can edit the map.

Editing rights expand progressively based on your [editor level](/en/editors/wme/niveaux).

## Waze servers

Waze operates on three separate servers. Switzerland is on the **ROW** server.

| Server | Coverage |
|---------|-----------|
| NA | North America |
| IL | Israel |
| **ROW** | Rest of the world, including **Switzerland** |

::: important Important
When in doubt, never edit the map based on the rules of a neighbouring country. Ask your question on the [local forum](https://www.waze.com/discuss/c/editors/switzerland/romandie/4797).
:::

## Permalink

The WME offers a permalink feature that lets you share a precise location on the map. Very useful for asking for help on the forum or Discord.

To obtain it: position the map over the desired area, then click the <kbd class="map-btn">🔗</kbd> button (the 3rd circle from the top, on the right of the editor)
