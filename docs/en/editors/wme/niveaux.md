# Editor levels

Waze uses a level system (L1 to L6+) that determines the editing radius and the accessible segments.

<!--@include: ../../_shared/niveaux-editeur.md-->

Segments locked at a level higher than yours require an unlock request via the forum or Discord.

## How to reach each level

In Switzerland, progression follows a "Community Ladder" defined by community consensus. Progression is **cumulative**: each level assumes mastery of everything expected at the lower levels.

From L4 onwards, the criteria fall into four families, marked by a colour: **editing skills**, **soft skills**, **community contribution** and **general**.

### L1 → L2

Automatic once you reach **3,000 edits**.

### L2 → L3

Automatic once you reach **25,000 edits**. An early promotion is still possible for an editor who produces consistently high-quality edits, masters the WME and best practices, and actively participates in the community.

### L3 → L4: The reliable contributor

Granted on criteria (staff approval + local champions). The 10 criteria:

<div class="cat-group cat-edit">

**Editing skills**

- **Area Manager**: regular maintenance of your area (accurate segments/places/house numbers), prompt handling of URs and MPs, tracking of road changes
- **Closures**: mastery of road closures (or completed training)
- **Junction Box (JB)**: understanding their impact on routing (or completed training)
- **Lane Guidance**: ability to add lane guidance and navigation instructions
- **Suggest an Edit**: moderating suggestions with professional communication
- **Permanent Hazards**: a proven record of adding permanent hazards locally
- **School Zones**: knowing how to define the polygons correctly

</div>

<div class="cat-group cat-soft">

**Soft skills**

- **Forum**: stay proactively informed and contribute feedback
- **Chat / local groups**: active and frequent participation

</div>

<div class="cat-group cat-community">

**Community contribution**

- **Community endorsement**: endorsement from local leadership

</div>

### L4 → L5: The community mentor

9 criteria:

<div class="cat-group cat-edit">

**Editing skills**

- **Lanes on JBs**: mapping lane guidance on junctions
- **Paths (Far Lanes)**: adding paths and configuring turns
- **Restrictions**: extensive understanding of restrictions (days, hours, vehicles, permits, lanes, tolls)

</div>

<div class="cat-group cat-soft">

**Soft skills**

- **Crisis response & shelters**: acting as a vital link to identify shelters during a crisis
- **Mentoring**: active 1:1 guidance or a trusted advisor role

</div>

<div class="cat-group cat-community">

**Community contribution**

- **New editor retention**: identifying and welcoming newcomers
- **Welcoming newcomers**: leading outreach with resources and support
- **Wazeopedia & knowledge sharing**: curating local documentation
- **Community endorsement**: endorsement from local leadership

</div>

### L5 → L6: The strategic leader

7 criteria:

<div class="cat-group cat-edit">

**Editing skills**

- **Request Imagery ("The Button")**: spotting outdated imagery and requesting an update

</div>

<div class="cat-group cat-soft">

**Soft skills**

- **Active in chat**: initiating conversations and relaying staff announcements

</div>

<div class="cat-group cat-general">

**General**

- **Leader & consensus builder**: demonstrating leadership through consensus building
- **Map raid organiser / project leader**: driving mapping initiatives
- **Relationship with the country coordinator**: a strong, collaborative partnership with coordinators
- **Additional active role**: holding a second role (Community Booster, Partner Coordinator, Mentoring Coordinator, Beta Tester, Coordinator…). See [Editor roles](/en/editors/wme/roles)

</div>

<div class="cat-group cat-community">

**Community contribution**

- **Community endorsement**: endorsement from local leadership

</div>

::: note Note
The selected criteria reflect the strongest consensus within the Swiss community. They are indicative: attribution remains at the discretion of staff and local champions.
:::

## Lock levels by road type

The lock level of a segment is distinct from the editor level. Swiss standard:

| Road type | Expected lock level |
| --- | --- |
| Freeway | L5 |
| Major Highway | L4 |
| Minor Highway | L3 |
| Primary Street | L2 |
| Street | L1 |

See [Routing basics](/en/editors/wme/routing) to understand the impact of road types on navigation.
